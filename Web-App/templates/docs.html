Before you start

The app keeps the books of a take-out and delivery pizza shop organised as a single-member LLC. The work is split in three:

  • You add the paperwork (scans and exports) and approve every entry. Nothing is posted without you.
  • Claude reads each document and proposes journal entries, with its reasons and any questions.
  • The app's code checks every figure, posts what you approve into a strict double-entry ledger, and computes the financial statements and tax worksheets. Claude never writes a statement or a tax figure.

Live books or a sandbox?

The dropdown on the right of the menu chooses which books are open. Live books are your real business. A Sandbox is a practice copy filled with made-up test documents; an orange bar under the top bar reminds you. Each set of books is completely separate: nothing you do in a sandbox touches the live books, and the live books refuse test files. Make sure the dropdown says Live books before you enter real paperwork.

Starting the app

In a command window: cd C:\Daya\RAGchamp\SMALL-BIZ-ACCTG\Web-App then python app.py, and open http://127.0.0.1:5050. The Claude Code app must be logged in on this computer.

1Set up your business (once) — {{ go('/setup', 'Setup') }}
  1. Business profile. Check the name, address, owner, EIN (kept on this computer, never sent to Claude), CT tax registration number, the date the books start and the tax year. Enter the last 4 digits of the business credit card(s) and the business checking account: this is how a business card is told apart from a personal one. Set the sales tax filing frequency (monthly, quarterly or annual, as CT DRS assigned). Click Save profile.
  2. Opening balances. Enter what the business had and owed on the start date: the bank balance, register and petty cash, inventory, equipment at cost and its accumulated depreciation, the credit card balance, sales tax owed, loans. Click Post opening balances. Do this before adding paperwork, or the bank will never reconcile. Made a mistake? Reverse the opening entry on the Ledger and post them again.
  3. Chart of accounts. The pizza-shop accounts are ready (sales, food cost, packaging, wages, rent, card fees …), each mapped to its Schedule C line. Add an account only if you need one; its description matters, because Claude reads it to choose accounts. Untick Active to stop using one.
  4. Vendor rules (optional). Standing instructions such as "Restaurant Depot: book paper goods to 5030". Usually they come from your feedback on the Review screen (step 3).
The Setup tile at the top of this page turns ✓ when the card and bank digits and the opening balances are in.
2Add your paperwork — {{ go('/inbox', 'Inbox') }}

What to add, as PDF scans or downloads: store receipts and vendor invoices, credit card and bank statements, checks you wrote or received, handwritten cash sheets, Square sales reports, DoorDash / Uber Eats / Grubhub statements and payroll reports. Square, bank and card exports can also be added as CSV (read exactly, without Claude).

Two ways in:

  • Upload: choose the files and click Add. Leave run the Bookkeeper after extraction ticked.
  • Inbox folder: drop the files into {{ inbox_dir }} and click ↻ Process the inbox. Added files move to processed\; refused ones go to refused\ with the reason.

The app then, for each file: refuses it if it was already added (under any name); makes page images; asks Claude to find every document in it (one page can hold several receipts) and read it; checks the arithmetic (items + tax = total, opening + transactions = closing …); and asks Claude to propose the entries. A small Working… box at the bottom right shows the progress.

Tips

  • Add things in the order they happened: receipts during the month, the bank and card statements after it ends.
  • A faint or small scan: click Re-extract (higher dpi) on its row.
  • Split PDFs longer than 12 pages before adding them.
3Review and post — {{ go('/review', 'Review') }}

Each document waits here with the scan in the middle and Claude's proposed entries on the right: the accounts, debits and credits, Why (its reason), any ❓ questions, any ✗ failed checks and ⇄ match hints.

ButtonKeyWhat it does

Feedback…FTell Claude in plain words what is wrong ("the TV was personal", "$40 was cleaning supplies"); it makes a new proposal. If it asks Remember this rule?, Yes, always saves a vendor rule.

Next / previousJ / KMove through the queue.
  • Bulk accept posts every proposal that is high-confidence, passed every check and has no question. Look at the Questions and Attention filters for the rest.
  • A figure Claude could not read shows as [?]: open Extracted data and checks, compare with the scan, correct it and tick I checked the flagged figures before accepting.
  • Review in order. A card purchase is first booked to 2150 Purchases awaiting statement; when the card statement comes, its line settles that amount instead of counting the expense again. The same idea keeps Square payouts, platform payouts, deposits and payroll from being counted twice.
4Check any document — {{ go('/doc-review', 'Input docs') }}

To see what became of documents, live or practice: tick Live documents and/or Sandbox documents, tick the documents and click Review. Each one shows the scan next to the data read from it, its ledger lines, the financial statement lines they feed and the tax lines they reach.

If something is wrong, tick what and write why, then Save feedback (kept as a file in Web-App\feedback\). Optionally re-process: re-propose with your feedback, read the scan again, or correct the figures yourself. A posted document is first un-posted by reversing its entries, and the new proposal waits on the Review screen for you to accept.

5The ledger — {{ go('/ledger', 'Ledger') }}
  • Every posted entry, newest first, with a link to its document and scan. Filter by dates, account or text; choosing an account also shows its running balance.
  • Posted entries are never edited or deleted. To correct one, click Reverse (a mirror-image entry cancels it), then post the right entry or send the document back through Review. Reversed something by mistake? Click Reverse on the reversal itself and the original is posted again.
  • New manual journal entry is for things with no document: the year-end inventory count, corrections. The panel also suggests the month's depreciation for equipment you capitalised (items of $2,500 or more); click Post it.
  • {{ go('/ledger-trail', 'Ledger trail') }} lists every change ever made to the ledger (posted, reversed, reinstated, months closed and reopened), with when and from which screen. It cannot be edited.
6Reconcile and close the month — {{ go('/reconcile', 'Reconcile') }}

After the month's bank and card statements are reviewed, pick the month. The app compares each statement's closing balance with the ledger (1010 bank, 2100 card) and lists the open items still waiting for a statement line.

  • Both ✓ → click Close. Nothing more can be posted into a closed month.
  • A difference usually means a document is still on the Review screen, a statement line has no entry, or a statement is missing. Fix it, or close with an override note explaining why.
  • A late receipt for a closed month? Reopen it (you give the reason; it is recorded), post, and close again.
7Financial statements — {{ go('/reports', 'Reports') }}

Choose a report and the dates, click Show; CSV downloads it for a spreadsheet, Print prints it.

ReportAnswers
Income statementDid we make money? Sales, cost of goods sold with the food cost % (watch 25–35%), expenses, net income. Option: compare with the prior year.
Balance sheetWhat the business owns and owes on a date, and the owner's equity.
Cash flowWhere the cash came from and went: operating, investing, financing.
Owner's equityContributions, net income and draws over the period.
Trial balance, General ledgerEvery account's balance; every line behind it (each links to its document).

Each report checks itself (✓ the balance sheet balances, ✓ ending cash ties to the bank and cash accounts, ✓ debits equal credits). The figures are only as complete as the paperwork posted, so reconcile first.

8Tax worksheets — {{ go('/taxes', 'Taxes') }}
Connecticut only. The tax worksheets assume the small business is based solely in Connecticut: it sells, collects sales tax and owes state income tax only in CT (Hartford here), with no locations, sales tax registrations or employees in other states. A business that also operates elsewhere needs those states' returns prepared separately with the CPA.
Drafts, not filings. The app prepares worksheets from your books for you and your CPA to review. Nothing is filed or paid by the app. The tax rules (rates, thresholds) are in tax_rules\<year>\ and should be checked against each year's forms.
TabUse it for
CT sales tax (OS-114)Each month's return: gross receipts, platform (marketplace) sales deducted, discounts, taxable sales at 7.35%, tax due and use tax, compared with the tax your books collected. Save this worksheet keeps a snapshot; then file and pay on myconneCT.
Schedule C / SEThe business profit for the owner's Form 1040, line by line with the accounts behind each, cost of goods sold (Part III), self-employment tax, an estimated-tax helper and the questions to settle before filing.
Fixed assets (4562)Equipment placed in service this year, for the CPA's depreciation choices.
1099-NECContractors paid by check or bank transfer; tick who needs a 1099.
ConnecticutThe figures for the owner's CT-1040.
CalendarEvery filing and payment date of the year (the next ones are also on the Dashboard).

At year end, click Download the CPA package: a zip with the general ledger, trial balance, statements, Schedule C/SE, the twelve OS-114 worksheets, the fixed-asset and 1099 lists and the open questions.

Monthly and yearly routine

Every week

  • Scan receipts, invoices, checks and cash sheets; add them (step 2).
  • Add the Square and delivery-platform reports.
  • Review and accept (step 3).

After each month ends

  • Add the bank and card statements; review them.
  • Reconcile and close the month (step 6).
  • Post the depreciation suggestion if you have equipment.
  • Look at the Income statement (food cost %).
  • Open the month's OS-114, save it, file and pay by the due date.

Year end

  • Count the inventory; post it as a manual entry.
  • Review Schedule C / SE, the 1099 list and the questions.
  • Download the CPA package and a backup.

Watch the Dashboard for

  • Documents to review and questions from the Bookkeeper.
  • Unmatched items older than 45 days (a statement line never arrived).
  • The next tax dates.
Practise with test data — {{ go('/test-data', 'Test data') }}
  1. Pick a sandbox in the menu's dropdown (e.g. Sandbox: jan-2026-scanned, a whole made-up January); the orange bar appears.
  2. {{ go('/inbox', 'Inbox') }} → Load the sandbox's generated files. This empties the sandbox and adds its files again, so you can review them yourself.
  3. Go through steps 3 to 8 as above. On Test data → Answer key you can compare with what should have happened.
  4. Switch back with Back to the live books in the orange bar.
Safety and backups
  • Everything stays on this computer, in Web-App\data\. Claude sees the document images and figures, never the EIN or full card and account numbers (only the last 4 digits).
  • Download a backup regularly: the link is at the bottom of the {{ go('/', 'Dashboard') }}, or on {{ go('/setup', 'Setup') }}.
  • Nothing posted can be silently changed: corrections are reversals, months can be closed, and the {{ go('/ledger-trail', 'Ledger trail') }} and the Setup screen's audit log record every change.
Questions and problems
If …then …
"… was already added on …"The same file is in the books already; nothing to do.
"… is SYNTHETIC TEST DATA …"A practice file can't go into the live books. Open a sandbox for practice.
A file shows failed on the InboxRead the error under its name; click Extract or Re-extract (higher dpi).
Accept says "confirm the flagged figures first"Compare the red figures with the scan, correct them if needed, tick I checked the flagged figures.
Accept says "no longer passes the checks"Something was posted meanwhile; click Refresh ↻ on the proposal.
"… is closed"The month is closed: reopen it on Reconcile (with the reason), post, close again.
The card or bank won't reconcileCheck the Review queue, the open items list and that every statement of the month was added.
An expense seems counted twiceUse {{ go('/doc-review', 'Input docs') }} on the receipt and the statement: the clearing line should say settled by ….
Words used
WordMeaning
Journal entryA dated record of one transaction: debits and credits to accounts that are always equal.
Debit / creditThe two sides of an entry. An expense or a bank deposit is a debit; a sale, a loan or a card balance owed is a credit.
LedgerAll posted entries; each account's running balance.
ProposalEntries Claude suggests for a document, waiting for your approval.
Clearing accountA holding account (2150, 1100, 1110, 1050, 2300) that keeps one amount from being counted twice until the statement or deposit arrives.
Open itemAn amount still waiting in a clearing account.
ReversalA mirror-image entry that cancels a posted entry.
ReconcileProve the books agree with the bank and card statements.
Accrual basisIncome and costs are counted when they happen, not when the cash moves.
Food cost %Food and drink purchases as a share of net sales.
OS-114Connecticut's sales and use tax return.
Schedule C / SEThe federal forms for the business profit and the owner's self-employment tax.
SandboxA separate practice copy of the books with made-up documents.
